This is the Golden Gate Bridge - an iconic landmark in the sunny state of California. This model, almost 4000 pieces, is my LEGO representation of such an amazing piece of architecture. I built it because I believed that such a famous, eye-catching landmark deserved to have its own LEGO. I believe in this project's potential as it's such a recognizable bridge and I think it would be a perfect addition to the LEGO Architecture set. I tried to capture the beauty of the bridge itself, whilst also adding the insane amounts of detail that sets every LEGO set apart from other generic building sets. From the cars on the road, to the smallest perfections, I just couldn't let any detail go. What do you think? Can we make it to 10K for my very first model?
